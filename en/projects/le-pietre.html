Le Pietre
Residential interiors
Castelfranco Emilia, Italy
2027, under construction

Le Pietre is a new residential building in Castelfranco Emilia, Italy, developed by Modena Ingegneria with architectural design by TT Architettura. The studio designed the interiors of the apartments.

Each room has its own character. In the living room a sage-green kitchen continues into a curved wall with round mirrors; in the penthouse the light-toned living area opens onto the terrace; in the bathroom large terracotta-coloured slabs wrap walls and floor.

The building is under construction, with completion expected in 2027. Renders: VISION.
