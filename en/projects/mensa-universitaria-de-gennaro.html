Mensa universitaria De Gennaro
Competition
Via De Gennaro, Naples, Italy
2026

Entry for the design competition launched in 2026 by ADISU Campania, the regional agency for university students' right to education, to convert the premises of a building on Via Pietro Jacopo De Gennaro in Naples into a university canteen with a cafeteria and study rooms.

The design organises the canteen as a courtyard scheme and the study spaces as a sequence of rooms. Circulation acts both as a connecting device and as a space to be used.

The courtyard holds the kitchen and the serving of meals, while the takeaway area sits near the entrance; the study rooms run along Via Marino and the cafeteria is on the first floor. Courtyard and rooms allow linear flows that keep crossings between routes to a minimum, and the spaces can be arranged in different ways without altering the structural scheme.

In the areas highlighted in dark red, ducts in the suspended ceiling distribute ventilation to the different rooms.
