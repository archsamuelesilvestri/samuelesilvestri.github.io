Via Carteria
Two apartments
Historic centre, Modena, Italy
2027, design
with Alessandro Orrea, geometra

On Via Carteria, in the historic centre of Modena, one apartment is being divided into two independent homes for two different clients. The design gives each its own identity.

In the first, the entrance is defined by a terracotta-coloured volume with a round mirror and a flush door, over a terrazzo floor. In the second, a blue volume frames a mirrored wardrobe, new red columns punctuate the corridor and chevron parquet leads to the dining room, where the existing decorated ceiling is preserved.

Design, interiors and bespoke furniture by the studio, in collaboration with Alessandro Orrea (geometra). Works planned for 2027. Renders: VISION.
