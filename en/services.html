Architecture services

The studio follows each project from the first site visit to the end of construction. It is based in Modena and works throughout Italy on homes, commercial spaces and historic buildings.

Apartment renovation

Design and site supervision to renovate homes and apartments, from the layout of the rooms to services and finishes. On Via Carteria, in the historic centre of Modena, one apartment is being divided into two homes.

Bars, restaurants and shops

Design of bars, restaurants, shops and commercial spaces: layout, bar counters and bespoke furniture, permits and site supervision.

Historic buildings and UNESCO settings

Work on historic buildings and in UNESCO settings, with sensitivity and respect for what already exists, including dealings with the heritage authority (Soprintendenza) where required.

Interiors and bespoke furniture

Interior design and bespoke furniture: kitchens, bathrooms, bookcases, bar counters. The studio designed the interiors of the apartments at Le Pietre, a new residential building in Castelfranco Emilia.

Building permits and site supervision

The right permit for each project under Italian building rules (CILA, SCIA or building permit), dealings with the municipality, and supervision of the works until completion.

Renders and virtual reality

Renders and virtual-reality walkthroughs to see a space before it is built and to make decisions together, in collaboration with VISION, a Modena studio specialising in architectural visualisation.
